Frequently asked questions

What are common 5 letter words ending with N?

AGAIN, BACON, BEGIN, BRAIN, CLEAN, GREEN, HUMAN, PLAIN, QUEEN, TRAIN and UNION are familiar examples.

Which N-ending pattern is especially common?

The -AIN family includes words such as AGAIN, BRAIN, CHAIN, GRAIN, PLAIN and TRAIN.

How do I search for a known N ending?

Put N in the fifth position and add every other confirmed letter. A full pattern will be far more useful than an ending-only list.
