How to narrow the answer

Do not treat every final D as a simple past-tense ending. BOARD, BUILD, CHILD and WORLD behave differently from BAKED or TRIED. In a crossword, enter the known letters as a full five-character pattern. In a Wordle-style puzzle, make sure repeated letters and excluded positions are also represented.

Use the right tool for the clue

Known positions belong in Crossword Solver. Green, yellow and grey feedback belongs in Wordle Solver. A rack of letters in no fixed order belongs in Word Finder or the Unscrambler. Keeping those clue types separate makes the result list much more reliable.

Frequently asked questions

What are common 5 letter words ending with D?

AHEAD, AVOID, BOARD, BREAD, BUILD, CHILD, FIELD, FOUND, GRAND, ROUND and WORLD are common examples.

Are most D-ending words also ED words?

No. Many five-letter words end in -ND or another consonant pattern, so the letter before D is an important clue.

How can I narrow an ending-D search?

Add the fourth letter first if you know it. -ED, -ND and other endings create much smaller candidate groups.
