Editorial and content quality policy

WordUnscramble.eu combines interactive word tools, reference lists and human-readable guides. This page explains what we publish, how we review it and where the limits of the data are.

What we aim to publish

Every indexable page should have a clear purpose for a person using the site: solve a specific word problem, explain how a feature works, teach a practical solving method, or provide a genuinely useful reference list. We do not intentionally create pages whose only purpose is to repeat a keyword with no additional utility.

Tool and word-list accuracy

The site uses open word data described on the How it works page. Results are generated from those lists and filters rather than being manually claimed as universally valid words. Different games and dictionaries can disagree, so branded-game legality should be checked against the current official rules for that game.

Guide writing and review

Guides are written around repeatable solving methods, concrete examples and the actual behavior of the tools on this site. We review content when tool behavior, word-list sources or important limitations change. Pages that no longer provide enough standalone value may be expanded, consolidated or removed from prominent navigation.

Corrections

Readers can report questionable word entries, broken examples or technical problems through the Contact page. Useful reports should include the affected page, word or search and enough context to reproduce the issue.

Advertising separation

Advertising does not determine word results, sorting or editorial conclusions. Interactive results remain available without requiring a click on an advertisement. Reference pages that are primarily machine-generated lists are intentionally treated more conservatively for advertising than substantial guides and core tools.

Independence and trademarks

WordUnscramble.eu is an independent site and is not sponsored by or affiliated with a commercial word-game publisher. Product names and trademarks belong to their respective owners.
