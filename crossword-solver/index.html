How to search a crossword pattern

Write every confirmed letter in its exact position and replace each unknown square with a question mark. A five-letter answer with R second and E fifth becomes ?r??e. Because the number of characters sets the length, there is no separate length field to manage.

Use pattern search before guessing the clue

Crossword solving usually works best as a two-part process. The clue tells you what kind of word the answer should be, while crossing answers tell you its spelling pattern. This tool handles the spelling side. The stronger your crossing pattern becomes, the more useful the candidate list becomes.

Common versus broad results

Common English keeps familiar words near the front and removes many obscure entries. It is the best default for general crosswords. Broad English is useful for specialist vocabulary, unusual proper-like dictionary entries, archaic terms or puzzles that intentionally use less familiar words.

Example

If you have a five-letter answer with R in position two and E in position five, enter ?r??e. If crossing letters or the clue also make S and T impossible, enter st under Letters to exclude. The solver will return words that match both conditions.

When the list is still too long

Do not add speculative letters just to force fewer results. Instead, solve another crossing answer and return with one more confirmed position. A single known letter in the right place can remove a large share of the remaining candidates.

FAQ

Can I use a dot instead of a question mark?

Yes. The solver accepts either ? or . for an unknown position.

Does this solve clue meanings?

No. It matches letter patterns against the site's English word data. You still need to compare the candidate words with the clue's definition, grammar and theme.

Why might the correct answer be missing?

The puzzle may use a proper noun, abbreviation, foreign term or spelling outside this site's word data. Try the broad word list, and check the puzzle's conventions if no plausible match appears.
