How do I solve an anagram from a phrase?

Remove spaces, punctuation and other non-letter characters, then enter the letters above. Start broad, then use exact length, Starts with, Ends with or Contains filters when you know part of the answer.

Phrase anagram solver vs. regular anagram solver

A regular anagram solver is usually best for a single word or short letter set. This page is designed for longer inputs from phrases, but it still returns individual words rather than automatically constructing complete multi-word phrase anagrams.

Tips for longer phrases

  • Start with uncommon letters because they reduce the number of plausible matches.
  • Use exact word length when the puzzle gives a fixed answer size.
  • Use ? for an unknown letter or blank tile.
  • Use pattern filters only for letters or fragments you know are correct.

FAQ

Does this create full multi-word phrase anagrams?

No. It finds individual English words from the available letters. It does not automatically split the full letter set into a complete multi-word phrase.

Can I use a wildcard?

Yes. Enter ? for each unknown letter or blank tile.

Why might a result not work in a specific game?

Games can use different dictionaries and rules. See How Our Word Data Works and verify disputed words against the relevant game's official source.
