What this word checker verifies

The checker answers a narrow question: whether the spelling you enter appears in the broad open English word list used by WordUnscramble.eu. A positive result means the spelling exists in that list. A negative result means it was not found there.

That is useful when you are checking a suspected anagram, crossword entry, vocabulary word or unusual spelling before using it in another search.

What a positive result does not guarantee

Different dictionaries and word games maintain different approved lists. A word found here is therefore not automatically valid in a particular tournament, classroom dictionary, Scrabble edition or Words With Friends ruleset. When game legality matters, the current official source for that game should be the final authority.

WordUnscramble.eu is an independent utility and does not claim to reproduce private or official game dictionaries.

How the word data is handled

The broad list is derived from open English word data and normalized for simple alphabetic entries between 2 and 15 letters. You can read more about the sources, limitations and corrections process on How Our Word Tools Work.

Word Checker FAQ

Can I check phrases or hyphenated words?

No. This checker is designed for one simple alphabetic word at a time.

Why can dictionaries disagree?

Dictionaries differ in scope, region, inflections, specialist vocabulary and editorial rules. Game dictionaries can be narrower still.

Does the checker send my word to a server?

The site loads its word data and performs the lookup in the browser. No account is required.
