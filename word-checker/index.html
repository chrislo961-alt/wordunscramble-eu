What does a Word Checker result mean?

A positive result means the exact spelling you entered appears in the site's broad open English list. A negative result means that spelling was not found in that list. The checker does not decide whether a word is valid in every dictionary, classroom, tournament or game.

Why can a game reject a word that appears here?

Word games and dictionaries use different editorial rules and approved word lists. A spelling can appear in a broad English source but still be excluded from a specific game's current lexicon. When game legality matters, use the official source for that game as the final authority.

WordUnscramble.eu is an independent utility and does not claim to reproduce private or official game dictionaries.

What is this checker useful for?

Use it to verify a suspected anagram, crossword entry, vocabulary spelling or unusual word before opening a broader search. If you have multiple letters rather than one completed word, use the Word Unscrambler or Word Finder instead.

How the word data is handled

The broad list is derived from open English word data and normalized for simple alphabetic entries between 2 and 15 letters. Read the source notes, limitations and corrections process on How Our Word Tools Work.

Word Checker FAQ

What does a positive Word Checker result mean?

It means the spelling appears in the broad open English word list used by this site. It does not guarantee validity in every game or dictionary.

Can I check phrases or hyphenated words?

No. This checker is designed for one simple alphabetic word from 2 to 15 letters.

Why can dictionaries disagree?

Dictionaries differ in region, editorial scope, inflections, specialist vocabulary and update schedules. Game dictionaries can be narrower still.

Does the checker send my word to a server?

The site loads its word data and performs the lookup in the browser. No account is required.
