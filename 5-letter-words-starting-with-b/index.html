How to narrow the answer

For B words, the second letter does most of the work. BL??? and BR??? lead to very different candidate sets, while a pattern such as B?A?D can be checked directly in the Crossword Solver. If you only know that B is present, use Wordle Solver or Word Finder instead of assuming it belongs in the first position.

Use the right tool for the clue

If every position is known or partly known, the Crossword Solver is usually fastest. If you have green, yellow and grey letter feedback, use Wordle Solver. If you have a rack of letters in no fixed order, use the Word Unscrambler or Word Finder. Keeping those intents separate helps avoid false matches.

Word lists vary between games and dictionaries. These pages prioritize familiar English examples for practical solving, while the interactive tools can search larger lists when a puzzle allows unusual words.

Frequently asked questions

What are common 5 letter words starting with B?

BEACH, BEGIN, BLACK, BLAME, BOARD, BRAIN, BREAD, BREAK and BROWN are familiar examples.

How do I narrow a long list of B words?

Add the second letter first, then any confirmed ending or fixed position. BL, BR and BE patterns remove most unrelated candidates.

Can I use these words for Wordle-style puzzles?

Yes. Use the examples for ideas, then move confirmed green, yellow and excluded letters into the Wordle Solver for a precise list.
