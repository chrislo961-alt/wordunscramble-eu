This page is designed for searches such as find words from letters, word finder for letters and find a word with letters. If you only need a quick list from scrambled letters, the main Word Unscrambler is usually enough.

What is a word finder?

A word finder combines a letter search with pattern filters. It is most useful when you know some letters, a target length or part of the word but not the full answer.

Unlike a basic unscrambler, Word Finder is designed to narrow possibilities. You can require a starting sequence, ending, contained sequence or exact length while still searching from the letters you entered.

Word Finder vs Word Unscrambler vs Anagram Solver

Use Word Finder when pattern clues matter, Word Unscrambler for a fast list from a rack of letters, and Anagram Solver when you specifically want rearrangements of the same letters.

Which word list should I use?

Common English is the best default for familiar words. Broad English maximizes coverage, while ENABLE is a public word list that can be useful as a separate reference.

Word Finder FAQ

How do I find words from letters?

Enter the letters you have, then use optional filters for exact length, starts with, ends with or contained letters. Use ? or * for unknown letters.

What is a word finder?

A word finder searches for words from a letter set and optional pattern filters such as exact length, starts with, ends with or contains.

Can I use more than one wildcard?

Yes. Each ? or * represents one blank letter, up to the 15-character input limit.

Why do I see unusual words in Broad English?

The broad list is intentionally inclusive. For everyday answers, switch back to Common English, where many less familiar entries are filtered out.

What if I know positions instead of a letter pool?

Use the Crossword Solver for exact letter-position patterns, or the Wordle Solver for five-letter clue states.

Is Word Finder free?

Yes. Word Finder is free to use, does not require an account and runs its main search in your browser.
