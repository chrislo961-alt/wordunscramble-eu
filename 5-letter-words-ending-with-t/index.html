How to narrow the answer

Use the fourth letter before scanning the whole list. A known S gives you -ST candidates, while N points toward -NT. If you already know two or three positions, switch to Crossword Solver and enter the exact pattern. Wordle Solver is better when letters are known but their positions are still uncertain.

Frequently asked questions

What are common 5 letter words ending with T?

ABOUT, ALERT, BUILT, COURT, EVENT, FIRST, GREAT, HEART, LIGHT, POINT, SHORT and TRUST are common examples.

What are useful T-ending patterns?

-ST and -NT are both frequent. Words such as FIRST, TRUST, EVENT, PAINT and POINT belong to these groups.

Can a T-ending list help with Wordle?

Yes, especially when the final T is confirmed. Add yellow letters, excluded letters and any other green positions to narrow the candidates further.
