Word list FAQ

What is the fastest way to narrow a word list?

Start with word length, then add a known starting letter, ending, required letter or exact position. When several clues are known, a solver is usually faster than browsing.

Are these official game dictionaries?

No. The lists use the site's documented open English word data and are not presented as official tournament dictionaries.

When should I use Word Finder instead?

Use Word Finder when you know exact letters, a required length, or a beginning, ending or contained sequence and want filtered results immediately.
