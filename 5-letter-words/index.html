Choose the right 5 letter word tool

Use the Wordle Solver when you have green, yellow and grey clues. Use the Crossword Solver for a fixed pattern such as ?A?ER. Use the Word Unscrambler when you have a rack of letters, and the 5 Letter Anagram Solver when every letter should be used exactly once.

Common words or a broader dictionary?

The common-word mode is usually the better first choice when you want familiar everyday answers. A broader dictionary is useful for word games and unusual vocabulary, but it can include words that are uncommon in ordinary writing. Our word-list guide explains the difference in more detail.

A practical pattern example

Suppose you know a five-letter answer starts with BR and ends with D. Instead of browsing hundreds of words, enter those constraints in Word Finder. If the clue is positional rather than prefix-based, Crossword Solver is usually faster.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find five-letter words with known positions?
Use Crossword Solver for a pattern or Wordle Solver if the clues come from Wordle-style coloured tiles.

Can I find five-letter anagrams?
Yes. The dedicated 5 Letter Anagram Solver checks full five-letter rearrangements, while the general Unscrambler can also return shorter words.

Why do different tools show different words?
Dictionary choice and game rules vary. Common-word mode prioritizes familiar vocabulary; broader lists include more specialist and unusual entries.
