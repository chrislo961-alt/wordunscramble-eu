How to narrow 4 letter words

If you know the first letter, last letter or a fixed position, use the Word Finder instead of scanning a long list. For example, a crossword pattern such as C?RE can be reduced immediately, while a rack such as RATE is better handled by the Word Unscrambler.

Frequently asked questions

Are all four-letter words valid in every word game?
No. Different games and dictionaries use different accepted word lists.

Can I search by pattern?
Yes. Use Word Finder or Crossword Solver when you know positions such as A??E or ?ATE.

Can I unscramble exactly four letters?
Yes. Enter the letters in the Unscrambler and set the result length to four.
