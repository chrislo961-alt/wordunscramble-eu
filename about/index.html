About WordUnscramble.eu

WordUnscramble.eu is an independent English-language word utility built to make letter puzzles, anagrams and word-pattern searches fast and easy to use.

What the site does

The site provides a Word Unscrambler, Word Finder, Anagram Solver and focused English word lists. The core search runs locally in the browser and does not require an account.

Word data

The current search index contains a large collection of simple alphabetic English entries between 2 and 15 letters. The project uses an open-source Wordnik word list distributed under the MIT License. The required license notice is included with the project files.

Word acceptance varies between dictionaries, word games and tournament rule sets. WordUnscramble.eu does not claim that every result is valid in every specific game.

Independent site

WordUnscramble.eu is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any particular board game, dictionary publisher or word-game company. Product names and trademarks belong to their respective owners.

Our goal

We aim to keep the site fast, readable and useful: the tool comes first, supporting explanations come second, and advertising—if introduced—should never make the tool difficult to use.
