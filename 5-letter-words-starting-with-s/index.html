How to narrow the answer

S often forms a consonant pair at the beginning. Locking the second letter therefore cuts the search sharply: SH??E, ST?R? and SC??? each lead to different groups. If you are solving a Wordle-style puzzle, record yellow-position restrictions as well as letters that must appear.

What are common 5 letter words starting with S?

SCALE, SCORE, SHARE, SHARP, SHEET, SHORT, SOUND, STARE and STATE are familiar examples.

How do I narrow hundreds of S words?

Start with the second letter, then add a known ending or fixed position. The Wordle Solver can also exclude confirmed grey letters.
