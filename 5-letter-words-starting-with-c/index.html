How to narrow the answer

Treat the opening pair as one clue. C followed by H, L or R creates very different candidate sets, while a known ending can reduce them further: C???K points toward words such as CHECK, CHICK, CLOCK or CRACK. Use only confirmed letters so a speculative filter does not hide the answer.

Frequently asked questions

Which 5 letter C words are useful in word puzzles?

CHAIN, CHAIR, CLEAN, CLEAR, CRANE, CRATE and CROWN are common choices with distinct letter patterns.

Why do CH, CL and CR have separate groups?

The second letter is a strong filter for C words. Grouping common beginnings makes it faster to compare candidates against known positions.
