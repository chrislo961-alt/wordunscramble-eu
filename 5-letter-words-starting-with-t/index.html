Use the right tool for the clue

If every position is known or partly known, the Crossword Solver is usually fastest. If you have green, yellow and grey letter feedback, use Wordle Solver. If you have a rack of letters in no fixed order, use the Word Unscrambler or Word Finder. Keeping those intents separate helps avoid false matches.

Frequently asked questions

What are common 5 letter words starting with T?

TABLE, TEACH, THANK, THERE, THING, THINK, THREE, TODAY, TOUCH and TRADE are familiar examples.

Why are TH and TR separated?

They are common but very different opening pairs. Treating the first two letters as one clue makes a large T list much easier to solve.

Can these results help with crosswords?

Yes. Combine the T start with crossing letters and enter the complete pattern in Crossword Solver for the most precise result.
