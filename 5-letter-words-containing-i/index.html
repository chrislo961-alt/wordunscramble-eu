How to narrow the answer

If I is fixed, write the whole pattern rather than merely requiring the letter. ??I?? and I???? lead to completely different results. In Wordle-style puzzles, a yellow I means it is present but not in that tested position, so record both the included letter and its blocked position. Repeated I is uncommon but still possible.

Use the right tool for the clue

If every position is known or partly known, the Crossword Solver is usually fastest. If you have green, yellow and grey letter feedback, use Wordle Solver. If you have a rack of letters in no fixed order, use the Word Unscrambler or Word Finder. Keeping those intents separate helps avoid false matches.

Frequently asked questions

What are common 5 letter words containing I?

BEGIN, BLIND, BUILD, CHILD, DRIVE, FIRST, LIGHT, NIGHT, POINT, PRICE and SMILE are familiar examples.

How do I find I in a specific position?

Use Crossword Solver with question marks for unknown letters. For example, ??I?? requires I in the middle position.

Should I use common or broad word lists?

Start with common English for most puzzles. A broad list is useful when the clue expects rare, regional or specialist vocabulary.
