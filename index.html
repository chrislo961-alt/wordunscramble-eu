Word unscrambler FAQ

What is a word unscrambler?

A word unscrambler checks which real words can be built from the letters you enter. It is useful for anagrams, vocabulary puzzles and letter games.

Can I use wildcards or blank tiles?

Yes. Enter a question mark for each unknown letter or blank tile, such as QU?TZ.

Does it work for Scrabble and Words With Friends?

Yes. Dedicated game finders provide focused results. Point values are a guide; official dictionaries can differ by game, country and edition.

Can I find words of an exact length?

Yes. Open Advanced filters and choose 2–15 letters. You can also require a beginning, ending or contained sequence.

Is WordUnscramble.eu free?

Yes. Every solver is free and works without an account. The main search runs locally in your browser.
