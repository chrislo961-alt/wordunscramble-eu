FAQ

Can I use wildcards?

Yes. Enter ? for each wildcard.

How are results sorted?

Choose longest first, highest points or alphabetical order.

Why use WordUnscramble.eu?

The site is built around a fast local word-search engine, a large English word index and simple filters. No account is required.

Word-list transparency

Our word data is based on an open-source Wordnik word list. Because dictionaries and game word lists differ, results should be checked against the rules of the specific game or dictionary when official validity matters.

Independent and easy to navigate

WordUnscramble.eu is an independent utility. You can move directly between the Unscrambler, Word Finder, Anagram Solver and focused word lists from the main navigation.
